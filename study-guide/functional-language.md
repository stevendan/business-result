Gom toàn bộ mẫu câu giao tiếp của 15 unit, sắp theo **tình huống công việc** thay vì theo unit.

## 1. Gặp gỡ & làm quen

**Giới thiệu bản thân** — Hi / Good evening. I'm … · Let me introduce myself. ·
I don't think we've met before. · Please, call me (tên).

**Đáp lại** — Nice to meet you, too. · I'm very pleased to meet you. · I'm delighted to meet you.

**Giới thiệu người khác** — This is … · I want you to meet … *(thân)* ·
I'd like to introduce you to … *(trang trọng)*

**Hỏi thông tin** — What do you do, exactly? · Where are you from? · Which part of … are you from?

**Xưng hô** — How would you like me to address you? · Is it OK if I call you …? ·
Do you mind if I call you …? · I'd prefer it if you call me …

**Giữ liên lạc & kết thúc** — Do you have a card? · I'd like to keep in contact. ·
I'm afraid I have to go now. · It was nice meeting you. · I look forward to hearing from you.

→ [Unit 1](unit-01-working-life.md), [Unit 6](unit-06-guests-visitors.md)

## 2. Điện thoại

**Nêu lý do gọi** — The reason I'm calling is … · I'm calling to arrange … ·
It's about our meeting next …

**Xin thông tin liên hệ** — Could you give me her details? · Can I have his number? ·
What's your web address? · Do you have his email?

**Đọc số & email** — `00` = *double zero / double oh* · `@` = *at* · `.` = *dot* ·
`/` = *slash* · `_` = *underscore* · `-` = *dash* · *lower case* / *UPPER CASE*

**Xác nhận & xin nhắc lại** — So that's … · Do you spell that with one N or double N? ·
A for apple. / A as in apple. · A not E. · Can you repeat that? · Is that E-R or A-R?

→ [Unit 2](unit-02-work-life-balance.md)

## 3. Hẹn & đổi lịch

**Đề xuất** — Can we arrange a meeting? · Can we fix a date to meet? · What / How about …? ·
Is … convenient? · Does … suit you?

**Đồng ý** — Yes, of course. · That suits me. · That's good for me.

**Từ chối** — I'm afraid I can't come on … · I'm sorry but I can't make it. · I'm not free on …

**Đổi lịch** — Can we make it later? · Can we **move it back** to Friday? *(lùi lại)* ·
Can we **bring it forward** to Wednesday? *(dời lên sớm)*

**Xác nhận** — So that's Tuesday at three. · We've confirmed Monday at 10 a.m.

> Khi nhắc lại để xác nhận (*On Monday? At three?*) → **lên giọng**.

→ [Unit 5](unit-05-customers.md)

## 4. Đón tiếp khách

**Chào mừng** — Welcome to … · On behalf of …, welcome to … · It's nice to meet you in person.

**Hỏi hành trình** — How was your journey? · Did you have any trouble finding us?

**Hiếu khách** — Come this way. · Can I get you a coffee? · Let me take your coat. · Have a seat.

**Giới thiệu lịch** — I'll run through today's programme. · First of all, I thought you could … ·
We'll catch up again later.

**Dặn dò** — You'll need this … · Make sure you … · Don't worry about …

## 5. Họp

**Hỏi tiến độ** — Can you update me on …? · What's the progress on …? ·
How's everything going? · Where are we with …?

**Báo cáo** — We've done / finished / completed … · So far, so good. · Everything's on track. ·
We're currently (+ V-ing) …

**Giao việc** — Is that something you can help with? · Would you like to help with that? ·
I'd like you to help …

**Nhận / từ chối việc** — Yes, no problem. · I'll do it. · Sorry, but … · I'd do it, but … ·
I'm afraid I can't do it.

**Chốt** — So, let's check we all know what we're doing … · You're going to … and I'm going to … ·
Let's meet again in two weeks to review the progress.

→ [Unit 3](unit-03-projects.md)

## 6. Họp trực tuyến

**Bắt đầu** — (tên) is here with me. · (tên), are you there? · We're just waiting for …

**Sự cố kỹ thuật** — Can you hear me OK? · Your line isn't very good. · Can you speak up?

**Điều phối** — OK, let's begin. Today, I want to discuss … ·
(tên), can you speak first? And then (tên), you can speak next. · Would you like to comment?

**Xin nói xen** — This is (tên). Can I ask a question? · Can I come in here? ·
Can I say something here?

**Kết thúc** — I think that covers everything. · Are there any questions before we finish? ·
I'll sum up the main points in an email. · Thank you everyone for coming.

> Ba thói quen: **xưng tên** trước khi nói · chủ trì **chỉ định tuần tự** · chốt bằng **email**.

→ [Unit 7](unit-07-working-online.md)

## 7. Thuyết trình

**Mở đầu** — Good morning and thanks for coming. · I'm here today to … ·
My name's … and I'm going to tell you about … · I'd like to begin by (+ V-ing) …

**Nêu bố cục** — First, I'll … Then, we'll … And finally … ·
Feel free to ask questions. **hoặc** I'll take questions at the end.

**Dẫn vào biểu đồ** — Take a look at this chart. · As you can see from this … ·
This chart shows / illustrates / represents …

**Nêu nguồn** — It's based on figures / data from … · They're from a survey of …

**Giải thích** — It's divided / split into … · The green line indicates … ·
This upward / downward trend shows …

**Hướng chú ý** — I'd like to draw your attention to … · Notice in particular … ·
This figure represents …

**Dự báo** — This increase will definitely continue … · This is going to fall in the future. ·
It will rise / fall / increase / decrease …

**Kết thúc** — That's everything I want to say. · That brings me to the end of my presentation. ·
Thanks for listening. · Are there any questions?

**Xử lý câu hỏi**

| Bước | Mẫu câu |
|---|---|
| Mời hỏi | Does anyone have any questions? |
| Không nghe rõ | Sorry, I couldn't hear you. Can you repeat that? |
| Không hiểu | Sorry, I don't quite understand the question. |
| Diễn đạt lại | Let me check I've understood you correctly. · You're asking me if …? |
| Bình luận (câu giờ) | Thank you for that question. · That's an interesting point. |
| Tách phần | I think there are two parts to your question. · First of all, … and for your second point … |
| Kiểm tra lại | Does that answer your question? |

→ [Unit 8](unit-08-finance.md), [Unit 12](unit-12-innovation.md), [Unit 14](unit-14-processes.md)

## 8. Đề xuất & khuyến nghị

**Đề xuất** — What / How about (+ V-ing)? · Why don't we (+ V)? · Maybe we should … ·
Perhaps we could … · Have you thought about (+ V-ing)? · We could always …

**Khuyến nghị** — I think we should consider … · It's probably worth (+ V-ing) … ·
I'd recommend … · It would be better to … · We might be better off (+ V-ing) …

**Nghi ngại / phản đối lịch sự** — It might prove (+ tính từ) … ·
I have a few reservations about … · I'd rather not …

**Phản hồi** — I really like it. · That's a good idea. · Great! · Exactly. ·
Sorry, but I don't think that would work. · I'm not sure.

> Sau *about* và *worth* → **V-ing**. Sau modal và *rather* → **V nguyên thể**.

→ [Unit 10](unit-10-facilities.md)

## 9. Đàm phán

**Nêu vấn đề** — I'd like to discuss an issue with you. · There's something we need to discuss. ·
We have a problem with …

**Lập trường** — My position is that … · The concern I have is that … · What's your position? ·
Do you have any concerns about …?

**Đề nghị** — Let me make you an offer. · What about if I offered you …? · Supposing we …

**Nhượng bộ** — In return, would you consider …? · **If I offer X, will you do Y?** ·
I'll meet you halfway. · I can't accept that, but I can offer you … · No, but how about if …

**Từ chối** — I'm sorry, but I can't agree to that. · I'm afraid that isn't possible. ·
I'm not in a position to accept that.

**Chấp nhận** — I think that would be fair. · I can agree to that. · It's a deal.

> Không bao giờ nhượng bộ mà không kèm điều kiện — dùng câu điều kiện loại 1.

→ [Unit 11](unit-11-decisions.md)

## 10. Xử lý vấn đề

**Hỏi vấn đề** — What's the matter? · How can I help? · So what appears to be the trouble?

**Trình bày vấn đề** — It **keeps on** (+ V-ing) … · It'**s always** (+ V-ing) … ·
It **won't** (+ V) … · It means that …

**Hỏi chi tiết** — What's wrong with it exactly? · What do you mean by …? ·
When you say …, do you mean …?

**Chẩn đoán** — It looks / sounds like … · It looks as though … · It could be … · It might be …

**Khuyên** — The best thing would be to … · I'd advise you to … · I think you should … ·
If I were you, I'd … · Have you tried (+ V-ing)?

**Chốt** — That should fix it. · That should sort it out. · That should solve the problem.

**Kiểm tra người nghe hiểu chưa** — Do you know what I mean? · Does that make sense? ·
Is that clear? — Đáp: Absolutely. / Kind of, but … / I don't get …

## 11. Đặt hàng & khiếu nại

**Đặt hàng** — I'd like to place an order. · This is a repeat order. · Please send them asap. ·
Please charge it to our account as usual.

**Hỏi thông tin** — Do you have an account with us? · Can you tell me your account number? ·
When did you place the order?

**Theo dõi đơn** — I'd like to find out about an order. · I'm chasing / following up an order. ·
Could you check it out for me?

**Giải thích** — We put it straight through to … · It says here that … · It was dispatched on …

**Khiếu nại — ba mức tăng dần**
1. I'm not happy about this.
2. This is a real problem for me.
3. This is unacceptable.

**Hứa xử lý** — I'll look into it immediately. · I'll find out. · I'll be as quick as I can.

**Hỏi lịch sự (câu gián tiếp)** — Could you tell me …? · I was wondering if … ·
Do you have any idea …?

## 12. Đánh giá & phản hồi

**Nhận xét chung** — Overall … · In general … · We're very pleased with your performance. ·
You seem to be doing very well. · One of your key strengths is …

**Nêu điểm cần cải thiện** — Perhaps one thing to work on is … ·
One thing I wanted to discuss was … · Is this an area you'd like to develop?

**Khuyến khích tự đánh giá** — How do you feel about …? · How are you getting on with …? ·
What do you think would help you to …?

**Tự đánh giá** — I'm really happy with … · Something I need to work on is … ·
I think … is an area for improvement.

**Đặt mục tiêu** — So let's summarize what we've agreed. · One thing you're going to … ·
You intend to … · You need to think about …

**Chốt đồng thuận** — How does that sound? · Is that OK with you? ·
Is there anything else you'd like to add?

**Khen ngợi**

| Trang trọng | Thân mật |
|---|---|
| You've done an excellent job. | Good job! |
| On behalf of everyone, I'd like to thank you for all your hard work. | Well done. |
| I'm very impressed by the results. | You're doing really well. |
| Your work is always of the highest quality. | Keep at it! |

> Điểm yếu không gọi là *weakness* — gọi là *an area to work on* / *an area for improvement*.

## 13. Câu đệm hằng ngày

**Thể hiện quan tâm khi nghe** — Really? · Is that right? · That sounds interesting!

**Nói "yes" — bốn mức**
Yes, absolutely! *(rất nhiệt tình)* · Yes, no problem. · Yes, sure. ·
⚠️ Yes, I suppose so. *(miễn cưỡng — người nghe sẽ nhận ra)*

**Nói "sorry" — năm tình huống**
Không làm được · Xin nhắc lại · Bắt người khác chờ · Nêu lý do · Ngắt lời
→ Đáp lại: No problem. / Sure. / Of course. / Not to worry. / That's OK.

**Trả lời ngắn** — Không bao giờ chỉ nói "Yes"/"No".
Công thức: `trợ động từ + thông tin thêm`
- *Yes, I have. I sent it this morning.*
- *No, not yet. I've been really busy.*

**Nói số ước lượng** — a little under · nearly · about / around · just over · well over
+ phân số: a quarter (25%) · a third (33%) · half (50%) · two-thirds (66%)

**Giải thích theo trình tự** — Begin by (+ V-ing) … · then … · After that, … ·
Once you've (+ V3) … · before you finish.

**Khái quát vs cụ thể** — Overall / In general / On the whole ↔ In particular / Specifically
